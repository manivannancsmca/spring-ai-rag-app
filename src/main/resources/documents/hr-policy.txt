ACME CORPORATION HR POLICY

1. Working Hours

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Employees must be available during the core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM
to 4:00 PM.

2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least five
working days before the planned leave date.

Emergency leave may be requested on the same day. The employee must notify
the manager as soon as possible.

3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate may be required for sick leave lasting more than
three consecutive working days.

4. Parental Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Eligible employees are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
Employees should contact HR at least thirty days before the expected leave
start date whenever reasonably possible.

5. Remote Work

Remote work is available to eligible employees based on role requirements,
business needs, and manager approval.
Employees working remotely must maintain a secure internet connection and
protect company data from unauthorized access.

6. Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense management system
within 30 days of the expense date.
Receipts are required for all expenses above INR 1,000.
Expenses must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.

7. Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice each year.
Employees receive feedback from their manager and may submit comments
within ten working days of receiving the review.

8. Code of Conduct

Employees must behave professionally and respectfully.
Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, fraud, and misuse of company
resources are prohibited.
Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

9. Data Security

Employees must use company-approved devices and software for business
activities.
Company credentials must not be shared with other individuals.
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the IT security team
immediately.

10. Policy Questions

Employees who need clarification should contact HR through the official
HR helpdesk.
This document is a general internal policy summary and does not replace
employment contracts or applicable law.
